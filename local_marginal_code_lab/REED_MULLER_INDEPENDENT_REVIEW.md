# Independent Reed--Muller review

Reconstructed the RM(s,2s+1) evaluation rows, rank, self-orthogonality, dual basis, affine minimum-weight witness, exact small word spans, and the level-two local support replay for RM(1,3). RM(2,5) is exactly enumerated over its 65,536 codewords; RM(3,7) uses the stated distance theorem because its 64-dimensional span is intentionally not expanded. The replay also reconstructs minimum dual supports through three pins for s=1..5 and checks the exact all-scope size inequalities through s=6.
