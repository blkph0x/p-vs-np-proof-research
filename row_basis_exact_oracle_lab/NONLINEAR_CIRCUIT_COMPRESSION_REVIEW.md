# Independent nonlinear circuit-compression review

A separate ripple-carry implementation exhaustively checks 32 small assignments and samples seven assignments from every k=7, 8 and 9 explicit family. It reproduces the exact gate counts and Hamming weight with 14, 16 and 18 output bits. This confirms a polynomial-size circuit-computable nonlinear boundary control; integrating the circuit into a SAT solver remains outside the result.
