# Independent connected affine review

The seven exhaustive small path instances were regenerated and checked with an independent affine-coset closure test. Their maximum contained dimension is exactly ceil(n/2). The Fibonacci recurrence and the all-size piece lower bound were independently recomputed through n=256; the n=256 lower bound is 1,090,151,200,392,118. Source hashes match. The result applies only to nonnegative affine-support decompositions and does not claim SAT hardness or a P-versus-NP resolution.
